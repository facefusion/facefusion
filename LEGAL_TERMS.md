Period
------

The challenge runs from October 1, 2026 to October 31, 2026. Entries published or tagged after the deadline are not considered.

- Participants must be at least 18 years old and of legal age in their jurisdiction

Submission
----------

- The project runs against the unmodified `v4-challenge` branch of FaceFusion
- The project is published as a public GitHub repository with setup instructions and a short demo video
- The repository carries the topic `facefusion-v4-challenge`
- The GitHub profile of the participant or team lead provides a valid email address
- The repository stays public and at its last commit before the deadline until the winners are announced
- AI assisted code is welcome, the tools used are disclosed in the README


Judging
-------

- Winners are picked by community vote and a jury of six people, both weighted equally
- The community vote takes place on Discord after the deadline, one vote per account
- The jury scores entries for whether they work, usefulness, API coverage, craft, originality and beta feedback
- Fake, purchased or coordinated votes are removed, involved participants are disqualified
- In case of a tie, the jury score decides
- The final ranking is determined by the jury and is binding

- To receive the prize, winners present their project in a short live call with the FaceFusion team
- Winners must respond within 14 days after being contacted, otherwise the prize passes to the next entry
- If a winner declines the live call or is not eligible, the prize passes to the next entry
- Team prizes are paid to one member of the team, the split is the responsibility of the team
